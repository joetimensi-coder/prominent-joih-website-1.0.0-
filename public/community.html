FAQ

Common questions

How do I choose a product?

Tell us your crop, area and goal, such as flowering support or soil biology, and we will recommend the best fit.

How should I store the product?

Keep it sealed in a cool, dry place away from sunlight, food and children.

Can I place a large order?

Yes. Send an inquiry with the product and quantity you need.

Will I see results immediately?

Results depend on crop, soil and weather. Trial a small area first and follow the recommended timing.

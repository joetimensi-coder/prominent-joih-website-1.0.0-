Information you can rely on

Safe, effective use starts with clear information. Each product lists its guaranteed analysis, source materials and recommended application so you know exactly what you are applying. Ask us about documentation for a specific product.

Good practice

Handling and storage

Store properly

  • Keep sealed, cool and dry
  • Avoid direct sunlight
  • Store away from food and feed

Handle with care

  • Wear gloves when mixing
  • Avoid inhaling powder
  • Wash hands after use

Keep out of reach

  • Store away from children
  • Keep pets and livestock clear
  • Dispose of empty packs responsibly

Safe use

Apply responsibly

  • Read and follow the product label and dosage guidance.
  • Do not exceed recommended rates; more is not better.
  • Trial a new product on a small area before wide use.
  • Follow local rules for fertilizer use and food-crop safety.
  • Contact us if you are unsure about mixing or timing.

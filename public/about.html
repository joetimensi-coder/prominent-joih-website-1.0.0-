Who we are

Organic crop nutrition, supplied since 2017

PROMINENT JOIH Organic Products, Inc was established in 2017 and is based in Magboro, Ogun State, Nigeria. We supply organic fertilizers and soil-health inputs to growers who want strong, consistent harvests without depending on harsh chemical inputs.

Our focus is simple: choose products with documented composition, explain them in plain language, and help each grower apply them at the right rate and the right time. Healthy soil is the foundation of every good harvest, and everything we supply is selected to protect it.

The science

How our products work

Healthy crops depend on healthy soil. Our range supports both: nutrients the plant needs, and biological activity that keeps soil fertile.

TAG K20: algae-derived potash

Potassium (K2O) from marine red algae supports flowering, fruit size and quality, and helps crops cope with drought and heat stress. Sulphur contributes to enzyme and protein formation.

BIOSOLTAMAX: microbial fertilizer

Organic carbon plus nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium, with a diverse microbial community that supports nutrient cycling and root development.

Reading the figures

What the numbers mean

  • N, P2O5, K2O: the main nutrients, shown as a percentage by weight.
  • C-Organic: organic carbon that feeds soil life and improves structure.
  • C/N ratio: carbon to nitrogen balance; it affects how quickly nutrients are released.
  • CFU/g: colony-forming units of living microbes per gram of product.
  • pH: acidity or alkalinity of the product.

Application

Timing and rates

TAG K20 is recommended at flowering initiation and during fruit development, at 48 g per acre as a foliar spray or 96 g per acre through drip. Results vary with crop, soil and weather, so start with a small trial area. See full product details.
